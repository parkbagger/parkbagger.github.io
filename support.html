Support

Questions, bug reports, and parks that don't show up all go to the same place.

Writing in

Email support@parkbagger.app. Write and I'll see it.

For a bug, say what you did, what happened, and what you expected. Your iPhone model and iOS version help too. Both are in Settings → General → About.

Common questions

A park is missing.

Park data comes from OpenStreetMap, which volunteers keep up to date. Search for the park by name and add it yourself. If it still doesn't show up, email the park's name and town.

Why is the travel time off?

Travel times are estimates from straight-line distance, with an allowance for roads. They're good for comparing parks, not for planning to the minute.

Do I have to share my location?

No. You can add places by searching an address, and bag parks without GPS confirmation. The app only uses your location while you use it.

Will my bags come back on a new iPhone?

Yes. Your places, bags, ratings, notes, lists and settings are kept in your own iCloud. You can also export a copy from Settings.

How do I start over?

Settings → Reset all. It clears the app and the iCloud copy.

Someone buried treasure with an offensive message.

Report it from the treasure. The app hides it, stops showing you that person's treasure, and opens an email to support with the details. Reported treasure is reviewed and removed if it breaks the rules.

How do I remove treasure I buried?

Use Dig up on it. Turning Treasure off stops all Treasure network requests.
