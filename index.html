Park Bagger

Bag every park near the places you spend your time.

Peak baggers collect summits. Park Bagger does the same for parks. Save home, work or school, pick how far you'll go from each, and the app lists every park inside that range. Visit one, rate it, and it's bagged.

How it works

Pick a place, set a range, start bagging

  • 01Save your placesAdd home, work, school, the gym, or anywhere you spend time. Search an address or use where you are now.
  • 02Set a limitChoose a travel time, like a 15 minute walk or a 10 minute bike ride, or a plain distance. Each place gets its own.
  • 03See every park inside itThe app lists each park in range on a map. Sort by nearest, largest, unbagged or best rated.
  • 04Bag itWhen you visit, mark it bagged and rate it with stars or a thumbs up or down. If you're standing in the park, the app can confirm the visit by GPS.

All places

Your places add up to one area

Switch to All places to combine them. A park inside two ranges counts once. The app tracks two totals: how many parks you've bagged, and how many acres those parks cover.

Collections

Not just parks

Switch collections to bag something else. Each one keeps its own list, its own range, and its own count.

How busy a beach is comes from what's nearby: places to eat, shops, piers and boardwalks. It's an estimate, not a live count. Ranges are defaults. Change them from 10 minutes on foot up to 4 hours by car.

Treasure

Bury something for a stranger to find

Turn on Treasure and you can bury a virtual treasure at an exact spot in a park, with a short message. Anyone else playing stumbles on it when they stand right there. Walk into a park with treasure in it and the app tells you so, then gets warmer as you get closer.

Treasure is opt in, because buried treasure is shared with other players through Apple's iCloud. It can only be buried inside parks, never at home, and nobody sees who buried it.

Lists

Visit soon, or consider

Not ready to go yet? Put a park on Visit soon or Consider. You can also search for any park by name, even outside your ranges, and bag it or save it for later. Bagging a park takes it off both lists.

Your data

Your places stay on your phone

There's no account and no sign-up. Your places, bags, ratings and notes are stored on your iPhone and synced through your own iCloud, so a new phone gets them back. You can export them as a file any time.

To find parks, the app sends a map location and a range to public OpenStreetMap servers. It doesn't send your name or anything that identifies you. There are no analytics, no ads and no tracking, and the developer runs no servers and receives none of your data.

Good to know

Estimates and map data

Travel times are estimates from straight-line distance, with an allowance for roads. They're good for comparing parks, not for planning to the minute.

Park data comes from OpenStreetMap, which volunteers keep up to date. Some areas are mapped better than others. If a park is missing, search for it by name and add it yourself.
